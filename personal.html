Frequently Asked Questions

View All FAQ's

How do I open a savings account?

You can open a savings account online through our website by clicking "Open an Account", or by visiting any of our nearest branches with your KYC documents.

What is the minimum balance requirement?

We offer Zero Balance Accounts as well as standard accounts which require a minimum balance of ₹1000 depending on the account type you choose.

How can I reset my online banking password?

Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. Enter your registered email ID or mobile number to receive an OTP and reset your password securely.

Is my personal information safe with you?

Yes, absolutely. We use bank-grade 256-bit encryption to secure all your data. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.
